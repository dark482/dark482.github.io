Speech-driven 3D facial animation often entangles shared articulatory patterns with identity-specific motion. DXTalker factorizes these factors into shared articulatory prototypes and personalized dynamics for accurate lip sync and expressive motion.

**Authors:** Dongxu Shen<sup>*</sup>, Yujian Liu<sup>*</sup>, Shidang Xu, Xiaoli Liu, et al.
