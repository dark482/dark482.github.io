Gigapixel WSIs contain sparse diagnostic regions, so dense high-resolution sampling is computationally prohibitive. CDSR shows that a compact set of informative high-resolution regions, selected and reconstructed through cascaded dual-scale learning, is sufficient for robust slide-level representation compatible with standard MIL aggregators.
