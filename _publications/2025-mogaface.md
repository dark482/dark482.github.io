Two-stage Gaussian avatar pipelines often freeze tracked FLAME geometry while optimizing appearance, which suppresses fine facial detail. MoGaFace instead refines geometry and texture together during Gaussian rendering via momentum-guided consistent geometry and latent-guided texture attention.

**Authors:** Yujian Liu<sup>*</sup>, Dongxu Shen<sup>*</sup>, Chuang Chen, Zairan Wang, Linlang Cao, Fanyu Geng, Peng Cao, Shidang Xu, Xiaoli Liu
