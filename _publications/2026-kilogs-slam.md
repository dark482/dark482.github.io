KiloGS-SLAM is a monocular 3D Gaussian Splatting SLAM framework for kilometer-scale outdoor scenes. It addresses pose tracking failure and memory overhead in long-sequence reconstruction through image-texture-complexity-aware Gaussian initialization and multi-view-consistency-guided Gaussian densification and pruning.
