Most 3D Gaussian head avatar methods assume accurately calibrated multi-view cameras. In unconstrained capture, predicted poses are coarse, FLAME initialization becomes unstable, and residual pose error is absorbed into appearance. AnyAvatar removes the calibration requirement and reconstructs animatable high-fidelity Gaussian heads from unposed images via joint optimization of camera poses, FLAME parameters, and Gaussian attributes, with a tri-plane-based view-consistent appearance representation.
